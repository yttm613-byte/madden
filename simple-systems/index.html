Payment reminders, appointment reminders, “your order is ready” messages. They go out by text or email, on time, without anyone having to remember.

03How it works

It starts with a short call.

  1. A free consultation.

    You describe the busywork. We ask a few questions. Then we tell you plainly whether it’s automatable. Sometimes it isn’t, or it isn’t worth the money, and we’d rather say that on the phone than three weeks in.

  2. A flat quote.

    One price for the whole project, agreed before we start. No hourly meter. Hourly billing rewards slow work, and we’d rather get paid for finishing.

  3. The build, then the handoff.

    We build it, check it against your real numbers, and show your team how it works. A tool your team can’t use isn’t finished.
