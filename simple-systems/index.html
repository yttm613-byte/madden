01Services

If it’s repetitive, it’s my kind of problem.

Four kinds of work. Not sure which one you need, or yours isn’t on the list? That’s what the first call is for.

Books

QuickBooks cleanup

You know the signs. Uncategorized Expense is your biggest line. The bank account hasn’t reconciled since spring. “Ask My Accountant” is a junk drawer.

I catch the books up, reconcile every account, and organize it all so your CPA just reviews.

Runs itself

Workflow automation

Bank-transaction matching. Recurring entries. The monthly report pack somebody builds by hand on the 3rd. And not only the books: orders that get retyped, documents that need filing, follow-ups someone has to remember. If you do it the same way every time, it should run itself.

Dashboards and reports built from your own data: sales trends, cash flow, what’s actually profitable, where money is leaking.

Books are where I come from, not where I stop. Email sorting, document filing, order entry, reminders, follow-ups, a form that turns into an invoice. If it’s repetitive and follows rules, it’s on the table. If it isn’t, I’ll say so on the call.

02How it works

It starts with a short call.

  1. A short, free call.

    You describe the busywork. I ask a few questions. Then I tell you plainly whether it’s automatable. Sometimes it isn’t, or it isn’t worth the money, and I’d rather say that on the phone than three weeks in.

  2. A flat quote.

    One price for the whole project, agreed before I start. No hourly meter. Hourly billing rewards slow work, and I’d rather get paid for finishing.

  3. The build, then the handoff.

    I build it, check it against your numbers, and walk you through how it works so you can run everything yourself. A system only I can run isn’t finished.
