Arrows/WASD run · Space hikes, throws, spins · Shift sprints · Q/E juke · How to play ▸
Offense: Arrows / WASD to run — Up = downfield, left/right cut · Sprint Shift · Space to snap, throw, or spin · 1–5 pick receiver · tap Space for a touch pass, hold it for a bullet · Q·E juke · R stiff arm · Z dive (a QB slides) · B on runs = control a blocker · F fullscreen · V announcer voice · P pause.
Defense: you control one defender — Space switches to whoever's nearest the ball. Run them down, and Q/E dives for the tackle (extra reach; miss it and you're on the floor). Blocked? Q/E rips free of the blocker instead. Ball in the air? R goes up for it — time it right and it's yours.
Clock & kicks: T (or tap the clock) calls a timeout, three a half · Space while a punt comes down to you = fair catch · behind in the fourth quarter, Space on your kickoff = onside kick.
Call your plays, stop the CPU's drives, win on the scoreboard before time runs out.
Tip: turn your phone sideways for the widest view of the field.
Drag anywhere on the left to move · the red button hikes, throws, spins, or switches defender · JUKE cuts away from the nearest tackler (on defense it DIVES, or RIPS free of a block) · tap a receiver's number before the snap for a hot route.
